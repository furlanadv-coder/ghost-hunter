PRIVACY POLICY

Ghost Hunter for iOS

Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

This Privacy Policy explains how Ghost Hunter handles information when you use the application.

Ghost Hunter is an augmented reality entertainment application developed by Fabiano Carneiro Furlan.

2. CAMERA ACCESS

Ghost Hunter requires access to your device's camera to provide its augmented reality scanning and visual exploration features.

Camera images are used by the application to provide the Ghost Hunter experience and environmental analysis.

Ghost Hunter does not upload camera images to a remote server operated by the developer.

3. LiDAR AND DEVICE SENSORS

On compatible devices, Ghost Hunter may use LiDAR, depth information, motion data and other device sensors to perform spatial mapping, distance estimation and augmented reality visualization.

These technologies are used to generate features such as 3D mesh visualization and environmental scanning.

4. PHOTO LIBRARY

Ghost Hunter may request permission to add images to your photo library when you choose to capture and save a scan from within the application.

Images are saved only when you initiate the capture function.

5. PERSONAL DATA

Ghost Hunter does not require account registration, login or the creation of a user profile.

The application does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or other directly identifying personal information through the app.

6. DATA STORAGE AND SHARING

Ghost Hunter does not operate a developer-controlled cloud service for storing your camera scans or LiDAR environmental data.

The developer does not sell personal information collected through Ghost Hunter.

7. TRACKING AND ADVERTISING

Ghost Hunter does not use your camera or LiDAR data for advertising purposes.

Ghost Hunter does not use this environmental sensor information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

8. ENTERTAINMENT PURPOSE

Ghost Hunter is designed for entertainment and augmented reality experiences.

Anomaly indicators generated by the application are based on sensor data and software processing and do not constitute evidence or proof of paranormal phenomena.

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Ghost Hunter does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
