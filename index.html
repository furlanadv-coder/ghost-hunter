ABOUT GHOST HUNTER

Ghost Hunter transforms your iPhone into an immersive augmented reality exploration experience.

The app uses the iPhone camera, visual analysis and compatible device sensors to explore and visualize the surrounding environment.

On supported devices, LiDAR technology provides additional depth information and 3D spatial mapping.

VISION Visual exploration
GHOST Anomaly visualization
MESH 3D spatial mapping

COMPATIBILITY

Available features may vary depending on the iPhone or iPad model.

Advanced depth sensing and 3D spatial mapping features require a compatible device equipped with a LiDAR Scanner.

IMPORTANT NOTICE

Ghost Hunter is designed for entertainment and augmented reality experiences.

Anomaly indicators displayed by the app are generated through sensor data and software processing and do not constitute evidence or proof of paranormal phenomena.
